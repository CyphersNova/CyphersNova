A Community of Student Builders

Founded in January 2026, primarily by IIT Madras BS in Data Science students, CyphersNova is a collaborative, independent tech community focused on building, researching, and deploying projects.

We're a community of students who believe the best way to learn technology is by building real things together. Our members come from diverse backgrounds but share a common passion: turning ideas into working projects while developing skills that matter in the real world.

Note: CyphersNova is currently independent of IITM or any other institution, giving us the flexibility to experiment and adapt to our members' needs.

Getting Started

We welcome members at all skill levels. Whether you're just starting your coding journey or you have significant experience, there's a place for you at CyphersNova.

Project Difficulty Levels

Beginner

Ideal for those new to coding or the specific technology. Strong mentorship and documentation provided.

Intermediate

Requires some familiarity with programming concepts. Good opportunities to deepen existing skills.

Advanced

Involves complex problem-solving and specialized knowledge. Excellent for challenging yourself.

Your First Steps

  1. 1. Browse active projects and identify one or two that interest you
  2. 2. Join the project's WhatsApp group and introduce yourself
  3. 3. Check the GitHub repository for current tasks and documentation
  4. 4. Start with a small contribution to familiarize yourself with the workflow
  5. 5. Stay consistent with your contributions—regular small efforts compound into real progress

Our Community Culture

CyphersNova thrives on collaboration, respect, and a growth mindset. We believe in:

Learning in Public

It's okay to not know everything. Ask questions, share your progress, and learn from others.

Respectful Collaboration

We value diverse perspectives and constructive feedback. Disagreements are opportunities to learn.

Quality over Speed

We prioritize well-documented, thoughtful work over rushing to completion.

Ownership & Accountability

When you commit to a task, your team counts on you. Communicate openly if priorities change.

What Success Looks Like

6-Month Goals

  • • Ship at least 3 projects to a working prototype stage
  • • Grow to 50+ active contributors across all projects
  • • Establish clear project documentation and onboarding processes

1-Year Goals

  • • Deploy at least 2 projects in production or real-world testing
  • • Have members successfully leverage CyphersNova work in job applications
  • • Develop a sustainable leadership rotation system
